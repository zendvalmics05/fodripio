## 1. Executive Summary

This memo establishes the formal characterization of the demand-generating process across all five manufactured SKUs (`P1` through `P5`). The findings documented herein directly inform model selection for **Phase 2 (Forecasting Baselines)** and **Phase 3 (Machine Learning Forecasting)**.

### Key Analytical Findings:
1. **Stationarity:** Raw demand series for all products display non-stationary or trend-stationary behavior (ADF test $p > 0.05$). Differencing or trend-aware modeling (e.g., Holt-Winters / SARIMA with $d=1$, or tree models with lag features) is mandatory.
2. **Seasonality:** Strong 7-day weekly seasonality dominates all products (mid-week demand peaks, weekend troughs). Annual seasonality is present in products `P1`, `P2`, and `P4`.
3. **Exogenous Effects:** Promotions induce significant demand spikes ($+14.5\%$ to $+51.1\%$ lift), while price variations exhibit strong negative elasticity ($-0.32$ to $-1.51$).
4. **Cross-Product Correlation:** Positive cross-correlation ($r \approx 0.63 - 0.68$) exists across all SKUs, driven by shared calendar effects and holiday dips.

## 3. Detailed Time-Series Properties

### 3.1 Distribution & Variance Structure
- All product demand distributions show positive right-skewness ($0.40 \le \text{Skew} \le 0.87$) with light-to-moderate kurtosis ($0.03 \le \text{Kurt} \le 1.62$).
- Multiplicative Gamma-like noise is observed: higher mean demand correlates with higher variance ($\text{Std} \approx 35\% - 38\%$ of mean).
- Outliers represent extreme demand spikes caused by simultaneous promotional bursts and unexpected demand shocks ($0.0\% - 0.55\%$ rate).

### 3.2 Seasonality & Autocorrelation Structure
- **Lag-7 Dominance:** Autocorrelation functions (ACF) show significant spikes at lags $k \in \{7, 14, 21, 28\}$, confirming strong weekly periodicity.
- **Lag-1 Persistence:** Partial autocorrelation functions (PACF) show a sharp cutoff after lag 1 and lag 7, supporting $\text{AR}(1)$ and $\text{SAR}(1)_7$ component structures.

## 4. Modeling Strategy & Recommendations

Based on empirical evidence, we recommend the following model families for downstream phases:

1. **Phase 2 Baseline Models:**
   - **Seasonal Naïve (Lag-7):** Essential baseline due to weekly periodicity.
   - **Holt-Winters / ETS $(A, A, A)$:** Captures additive trend and multiplicative weekly seasonality.
   - **SARIMAX $(1,1,1) \times (1,1,1)_7$ with Exogenous Regressors:** Models non-stationarity while incorporating `price` and `promotion`.

2. **Phase 3 Machine Learning Models:**
   - **XGBoost / LightGBM:** Highly suited for non-linear promo-price interactions, calendar encodings, and lag features ($1, 2, 7, 14, 28$).
   - **Leakage Prevention:** All models must utilize features generated by [`FeatureEngineer`](file:///c:/zendvalmics/project/fodripio/src/data/features.py) with strictly shifted rolling windows ($t-1$ cutoff).
